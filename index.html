What We Offer

Calming but challenging yoga in an informal group environment — open to everyone, always free.

  • Yoga Classes — Regular, Intermediate, Easy, Chair, Kids, and Silent yoga classes for all ages and fitness levels.
  • Pranayama — Daily morning breathing sessions — our most popular offering, helping you start the day energized.
  • Meditation — Evening meditation classes to help you relax, unwind, and enjoy deeper more restful sleep.
  • Kids Yoga — Fun and engaging yoga for kids aged 4–14. Kids can earn SSL hours by leading their own classes!
  • Specialty Classes — Osteoporosis, Acupressure, Chair Yoga, Sun Salutations, and Juno Wellness by expert instructors.
  • Expert Sessions — Periodic sessions on Vedanta, Nutrition, Ayurveda, Parkinson's, MS, and more wellness topics.

About Shri Yoga

Making authentic yoga and wellness accessible to everyone through free online and in-person programs.

Shri Yoga is a Maryland-based nonprofit organization committed to promoting health, wellness, and mindful living by making authentic yoga accessible to everyone. We believe that yoga is a powerful tool for improving physical health, mental well-being, and overall quality of life, and that these benefits should be available to all without financial barriers.

We proudly offer always-free online and in-person yoga and wellness programs for children, adults, and seniors, creating an inclusive environment that welcomes participants of all experience levels and abilities.

Our comprehensive offerings include Yoga Asana, Pranayama (Breathwork), Meditation, Chair Yoga, Bone Builder & Osteoporosis Support, Sound Healing, and Wellness Education and Expert-Led Sessions.

Shri Yoga is supported by a dedicated team of more than 40 certified yoga teachers who are committed to delivering safe, evidence-informed, and accessible instruction. Our instructors bring diverse expertise and a shared passion for serving the community through high-quality yoga education.

Our mission is to empower individuals and strengthen communities by providing free, holistic wellness programs that foster physical vitality, emotional resilience, mental clarity, and inner peace. Whether you are beginning your yoga journey, seeking to improve flexibility and strength, managing stress, supporting bone health, or looking for a welcoming community, Shri Yoga provides a compassionate and supportive space where every individual can thrive.

Our Mission

To improve community health and well-being by providing free, accessible, and high-quality yoga and wellness programs for people of all ages and abilities.

Our Vision

To create healthier, stronger, and more connected communities where yoga and holistic wellness are accessible to everyone.

Founder & Lead Instructor

Shrikant "Shri Ji" Sabnis

Fondly known as "Shri Ji" and "Shri Kaka," Shrikant Sabnis retired after 40 years in safety-critical engineering to dedicate his life to teaching yoga and spreading love and compassion in his community.

For over 12 years, he has conducted free yoga at temples, community halls, volunteer homes, and online — traveling 70 miles a day and growing from local classes to 1,200+ global students.

  • Certified RYT 200 — OneAum Studio, Potomac MD
  • 500+ hours yoga teacher training
  • Trained in Sudarshan Kriya, Vipassana, 3-Step Rhythmic Breathing
  • Inspired 30+ certified practitioners
  • Featured by Voice of America

Classes

70+ free yoga classes every week for all ages, levels, and abilities. Online via Zoom and in person at Rockville, Germantown, Clarksburg, Urbana and SSVT, Maryland.

Class Types

From beginner-friendly to advanced — we have something for every body and schedule.

  • Regular — Full yoga with asanas, breathing & relaxation.
  • Pranayama — Morning & evening breathing sessions.
  • Intermediate — Advanced sequences for experienced students.
  • Easy / Chair — Gentle practice for seniors or mobility needs.
  • Meditation — Evening meditation for relaxation & sleep.
  • Kids Yoga — Ages 4–14. SSL hours for teen instructors.
  • Osteoporosis — Specialist class led by a Physical Therapist.
  • Acupressure — Pressure-point healing with expert guidance.

Before You Join

  • What to Wear — Comfortable, stretchy clothing. Yoga pants, leggings, or shorts. Bare feet preferred.
  • What to Bring — Yoga mat, water bottle, small towel. Online: stable internet & enough floor space.
  • Before & After — Avoid heavy meals 2 hours before. Stay hydrated. Rest briefly after class.
